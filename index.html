Wheel of A Course in Miracles

A spinning wheel that generates messages drawn from the teachings of A Course in Miracles, along with their corresponding references.

▶ Open the wheel
Free app – For personal use.
On a phone: in your browser’s menu, choose “Add to Home Screen” to keep the wheel handy, like an app.
If there is a problem with the hosting, you can also download the file and open it locally in your web browser on a computer.
